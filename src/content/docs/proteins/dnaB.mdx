# Protein Information

**Full name:** Probable replicative DNA helicase

A replicative DNA helicase, it participates in initiation and elongation during chromosome replication. Travels ahead of the DNA replisome, separating dsDNA into templates for DNA synthesis. A processive ATP-dependent 5'-3' DNA helicase it has DNA-dependent ATPase activity The intein is an endonuclease
