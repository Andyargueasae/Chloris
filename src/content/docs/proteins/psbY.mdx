# Protein Information

**Full name:** Photosystem II reaction center protein Y

Loosely associated component of the core of photosystem II (PSII), it is not always seen in crystals. PSII is a light-driven water plastoquinone oxidoreductase, using light energy to abstract electrons from H(2)O, generating a proton gradient subsequently used for ATP formation
