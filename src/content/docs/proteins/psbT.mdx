# Protein Information

**Full name:** Photosystem II reaction center protein T

Found at the monomer-monomer interface of the photosystem II (PS II) dimer, plays a role in assembly and dimerization of PSII. PSII is a light-driven water plastoquinone oxidoreductase, using light energy to abstract electrons from H(2)O, generating a proton gradient subsequently used for ATP formation
