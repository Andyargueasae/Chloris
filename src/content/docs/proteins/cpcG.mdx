# Protein Information

**Full name:** Phycobilisome rod-core linker polypeptide cpcG

Rod-core linker protein required for attachment of phycocyanin to allophycocyanin in cores of phycobilisomes Linker polypeptides determine the state of aggregation and the location of the disk-shaped phycobiliprotein units within the phycobilisome and modulate their spectroscopic properties in order to mediate a directed and optimal energy transfer
