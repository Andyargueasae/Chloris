## Scope of the database

Previous existing databases focusing on plastids or organellar genomes are metadata-rich with direct link to the source provider of the data like NCBI GenBank, but they are restricted at genome-level and do not summarize patterns and orthology at protein level, constraining the utility of the database for downstream applications. 
In contrast, our database is protein-level and orthology-aware, which allows us to capture the evolutionary history of plastid genes and their relationships across different species.

## Main resources

We aim to create a database that serves all aspect of molecular genomic plastid research, by offering these types of data:
1. HMMs in [HMMER3](http://hmmer.org/) format;
2. Orthogroups segregated in aligned FASTA format;
3. The phylogenetic tree in newick format showing a use case of conserved plastid genes across the tree of life;
4. Pfam-Protein mapping matrix in tab-delimited format;
5. Separated Unknown orthogroups we did not include in the website, but available for download and further exploration. 

## Use cases

The orthogroups and MSA of proteins, can be applied to the following scenarios:
1. Phylogenetic analysis of plastid genomes, by extracting desired markers and concatenating them for tree inference.
2. Sensitive protein sequence searching and clustering when cooperated with BLAST, DIAMOND or MMseqs2, to explore the diversity of plastid genes in metagenomic datasets.
3. Functional annotation of plastid genes, by mapping the HMMs to Pfam domains and inferring potential functions based on domain architectures.

The resulting HMMs can be applied to a range of tasks, including:
1. Quantitative taxonomic profiling with existing packages (e.g.: [singleM](https://wwood.github.io/singlem/)).
2. Estimating qualities of plastid metagenome-assembled genomes if used in metagenomic analyses.
3. Extracting desired markers when cooperated with orthogroup inference packages like [OrthoFisher](https://github.com/JLSteenwyk/OrthoFisher), to streamline the process of phylogenetic analysis of plastid genomes.
