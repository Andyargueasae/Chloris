---

Our fundamental insights were derived from a plastid phylogenetic tree: built from dereplicated genomes from 2250 plastid genomes from NCBI GenBank.

We firstly dereplicated these genomes using an ANI of 95% (we used galah, a bioinformatic tool), resulting in 898 plastid genomes with annotated proteins.

We then picked 51 genes that are ultra-conserved in these genomes, to infer a phylogenetic tree. These genes are mainly single-copy and are conserved across most genomes in the dataset.
