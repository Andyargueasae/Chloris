# Gene Information

**Full name:** Large ribosomal subunit protein uL22c

This protein binds specifically to 23S rRNA The globular domain of the protein is located near the polypeptide exit tunnel on the outside of the subunit, while an extended beta-hairpin is found that lines the wall of the exit tunnel in the center of the 70S ribosome
